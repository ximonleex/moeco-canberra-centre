Inside the shop

Bright chairs.
Bold style.

MOE & Co. began in 2014 as a family business with a simple idea: swap the plain, clinical salon for bright, fun spaces full of neon and its signature 90s-style poster wall — places where locals feel welcome and walk out looking sharp.

It started out as a classic barbershop, and that spirit lives on here at Canberra Centre. The focus is quality cuts at fair prices, no appointment needed, and a team that's trained and mentored to grow their craft.
